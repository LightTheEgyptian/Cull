# Changelog

## 1.1 — October 2026

- **Star ratings:** press 1–5. Ratings (and rejects) are saved to XMP sidecars that Lightroom and Capture One read.
- **Bursts:** shots taken within 2 seconds are grouped. Cull picks the best one (sharpest, eyes open, best composed), and **B** keeps it and rejects the rest.
- **Compare:** **C** shows a burst side by side.
- **Zoom to faces:** **F** frames the biggest face so you can check eyes and focus.
- **Camera info:** **I** shows camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed and ISO.
- **Export for clients:** full size, 3000 px or 2048 px JPEGs, with an optional watermark. Location data is removed.
- **Drag to browse:** drag or flick a photo sideways to move to the next one.
- **Undo** for marks and ratings (⌘Z), a Starred filter, a keyboard shortcuts sheet (⌘/), and an About window.

## 1.0 — October 2026

- First release: keep and reject with one key, focus and closed-eye checks on your Mac, RAW + JPEG pairs, and moving keepers and rejects into folders with undo.
